Our story

where our journey begins

Our first MikCaf café opens its doors in Bengaluru with a celebration of two much-loved food cultures – Mumbai and Gujarat.

Inspired by the vibrant streets of Mumbai and the comforting flavours of Gujarat, our first outlet brings together iconic street-food favourites, traditional tastes and contemporary café indulgences.

We want every visit to feel special – whether you're enjoying a nostalgic street-food favourite, discovering something new or simply spending quality time with friends and family.

Bengaluru is where our journey begins, but it is certainly not where our ambition ends.

One beginning. Many destinations. Endless flavours.

a menu built on variety & flavour

From the irresistible Vada Pav, Pav Bhaji and Dabeli to Gujarati-inspired delicacies, grilled sandwiches, refreshing beverages, shakes, faloodas and ice creams — designed to celebrate variety, flavour and the joy of eating.

Our vision

a global café brand

Our vision is to build MikCaf into a globally recognised café brand, bringing premium-quality food, diverse flavours and memorable experiences to people across cities and countries.

We envision a family of contemporary cafés, each with its own identity, local inspiration and unique culinary experiences, yet united by one common promise – exceptional quality, freshly prepared food, warm hospitality and accessible prices.

We believe premium café experiences should be within everyone's reach. Our ambition is to make great food an everyday pleasure rather than an occasional luxury.

With a passion for innovation, respect for local food cultures and a commitment to customer happiness, we aim to create a brand that grows beyond geographical boundaries.

From Bengaluru to the World. One MikCaf at a Time.

The passion behind every recipe

every recipe has a thought behind it

Every ingredient matters. At MikCaf, great food begins with great attention to detail.

Our recipes are thoughtfully developed and curated under the guidance of our founder, Anamika Desai – a nutritionist, home chef and food entrepreneur with years of experience in the food industry.

For us, serving delicious food is not just about taste. It is also about understanding ingredients, maintaining quality and ensuring that every dish is prepared with care. From selecting ingredients to the preparation process and the final presentation, every detail matters.

Even when it comes to indulgent favourites and fast food, we believe quality should never take a back seat.

Our philosophy is simple: serve food that tastes wonderful, is thoughtfully prepared and makes people look forward to coming back.

Because great taste deserves great care.

Chef-led · Founder-created · Passionately prepared

every recipe has a personal touch

Every dish has a story.

At MikCaf, food is personal.

Every recipe on our menu is personally created by our founder, Anamika Desai, bringing together her culinary passion, creativity and years of experience in the food industry.

As a nutritionist, home chef and food entrepreneur, Anamika pays special attention to the ingredients that go into every dish, the preparation methods and the quality of food served to our customers. From developing recipes and selecting ingredients to freshly preparing delicious food, she brings a hands-on approach to the MikCaf kitchen.

We believe fast food can be more than just quick and convenient. It can be freshly made, thoughtfully prepared, full of flavour and served with uncompromising attention to quality.

Whether it's an indulgent street-food favourite, a satisfying meal or a delightful dessert, every creation reflects our founder's passion for good food.

A note from our founder

Food has always been more than a profession for me; it has been a passion.

Having spent years in the food industry, including 14 years building my catering business in the UK, I have learnt that the secret to creating memorable food lies in the details – the ingredients we choose, the way we prepare our food and the love we put into every recipe.

As a nutritionist and home chef, I naturally pay attention to what goes into the food we serve. At MikCaf, this philosophy continues even when we create indulgent fast-food favourites.

I want people to enjoy their favourite foods without compromising on the importance of quality and thoughtful preparation.

My dream is to build MikCaf into a brand where great taste, quality and memorable experiences come together, wherever we go in the world.

Thank you for being part of this journey.

Anamika DesaiFounder, MikCaf
